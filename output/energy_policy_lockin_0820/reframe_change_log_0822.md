# Reframe audit log: Designed for Adjustment

This audit log is the marked companion to the clean manuscript. It records the important narrative changes without placing editorial annotations in a submission PDF.

## Title and abstract

- [Reframe: policy failure -> policy-design boundary] The title now identifies an adjustment-oriented design and a missing exit pathway.
- [Reframe: no transition -> different transition margins] The abstract reports the observed composition transition alongside continued coal-asset accumulation.
- [Reframe: criticism of first-generation policy -> incomplete long-run design] The abstract distinguishes the operating-margin rationale from the need for a second-generation exit framework.

## Introduction and theory

- [Literature integration: standalone map -> manuscript argument] The introduction now brings the phaseout, power-system, regional-transition, and green-finance literatures into the problem formulation. It uses these literatures to distinguish operating adjustment, composition, and asset exit, rather than treating citations as a separate background list.
- [Reframe: 2012 as treatment -> post-2012 as observation window] The Green Credit Guidelines are retained as institutional context, but not as an intervention expected to retire coal assets.
- [Reframe: generic carbon lock-in -> lock-in through adjustment] The theoretical contribution is that cleaning and optimizing incumbent assets can defer the coordination required for retirement.
- [Reframe: operational improvement versus exit as a result anomaly -> greening the stock versus exiting the stock as a policy-design distinction] The three margins are preserved: operating, compositional, and asset exit.
- [Reframe: linear policy escalation -> first- and second-generation transition policy] First-generation policy covers adjustment, cleaner utilization, renewable deployment, and adequacy; second-generation policy covers retirement, replacement, grid integration, and local adjustment.

## Results and figures

- [Reframe: hazard ratio near one -> policy ineffectiveness] The Cox estimates are now read as observational evidence consistent with an architecture that did not target differential fleet-wide retirement. They remain non-causal and imprecise.
- [Reframe: low shares imply exit -> composition is distinct from exit] New Figure 5 reports the annual composition of the five-energy final-use basket, installed capacity, and generation with their denominators stated explicitly.
- [Reframe: electricity equals clean energy] The Figure 5 note explicitly states that electricity in final use is not a clean-energy category and non-thermal power is broader than renewables.
- [Reframe: Shanxi and Inner Mongolia as two failed exits] The comparison is now presented as different regional configurations of the same adjustment-first architecture.

## Discussion and conclusion

- [Reframe: first-generation policy failed] The manuscript now states that first-generation policy can succeed on its own operating terms.
- [Reframe: missing exit as an implementation defect] The manuscript identifies a long-run policy-design gap: asset exit needs explicit retirement finance, replacement services, grid absorption and dispatch, and fiscal and labour adjustment.
- [Reframe: generic policy package] The four elements are packaged as the complementary components of second-generation transition policy.
