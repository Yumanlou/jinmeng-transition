## 中文综述

### 1. 现有转型研究解释了“为什么会改善”，却不足以判断“既有资产是否退出”

能源转型研究通常从碳锁定、路径依赖和社会技术转型出发，说明既有能源系统为何会在技术、制度、市场和行为层面相互强化（Unruh, 2000；Pierson, 2000；Geels & Schot, 2007；Markard et al., 2012）。这一传统的重要启发是，转型并不等于单一技术替代，也不必沿着线性路径发生。环境规制、能源价格、金融约束与创新政策可以改变生产方式、技术选择和能源使用强度（Acemoglu et al., 2012；Campiglio, 2016）。因此，煤炭强度下降、污染治理加强、可再生能源扩张和煤电利用小时下降，本身都是真实且重要的转型进展。

但这一文献也留下一个测量上的盲点：它们常用排放强度、燃料份额、装机份额或绿色投资衡量转型，而这些指标并不直接回答既有煤电机组是否退役。煤电资产可以在更低负荷、更高效率、更严格排放标准下继续运行；可再生能源也可以以“增量叠加”而非“存量替代”的方式扩张。因而，运行调整、能源构成变化与资产退出应被视为三个相关但不可互相替代的转型边际。本文正是从这个区分切入：它并不否认中国在前两个边际上的进展，而是要求用直接机组生命周期数据检验第三个边际。

### 2. 煤电退出研究已说明“应该如何退”，但较少诊断现实政策组合为何没有自动产生退出

近年的煤电退出文献已经把讨论推进到机组层面。Cui 等（2019, 2021）以巴黎目标和中国机组特征为基础，说明煤电长期运行与深度减排目标之间存在寿命约束，并提出逐厂排序的退出路径；Zhang 等（2022）进一步讨论早期退出与公平低碳转型的联系。Kefford 等（2018）和 Jewell 等（2019）则表明，提前退役不仅是技术或成本问题，也受制于政治可行性和资产寿命。Yan 等（2024）把不确定成本带入煤电转型决策，Yu 等（2023）则明确显示，排放交易和强制性退出规则处理的并不是同一个问题。

这些研究共同表明，煤电退出需要一个可执行的安排：决定哪些机组何时退出，如何处理债务、补偿、修复和替代服务，以及如何约束新增高碳投资。搁浅资产研究进一步提示，退出成本会在业主、金融机构、地方政府和劳动者之间重新分配（Johnson et al., 2015；van der Ploeg & Venables, 2020）。不过，现有文献大多是情景模拟、规范性排序或机制讨论，较少从历史观察出发检验：在一个已经实施效率提升、污染治理、可再生能源扩张和金融环境约束的政策时期，煤电资产是否真的出现更快退出。本文的增量在于补上这个诊断环节，而不是再做一套最优退出排程。

### 3. 中国电力系统文献构成必要的反论：煤电保留并不自动等于转型失败

关于中国电力系统的研究强调，煤电退出必须放在电力可靠性、跨区输电、储能、水电调节、调度规则和可再生能源消纳中理解。SWITCH-China 及后续研究显示，中国实现低碳电力系统在技术上依赖一组互补条件，而非单独增加风光装机（He et al., 2016, 2020；Liu et al., 2019）。Davidson 等（2016）说明，在煤电占主导的电网中，调度和并网规则影响风电能否真正替代煤电出力；Chen 等（2022）进一步讨论了弃风率下降背后的制度与系统变化。Wang 等（2023）表明，中国风光扩张潜力很大。

这组文献要求本文避免一个廉价结论：煤电存量仍在，就把它解释为“能源转型失败”。An 等（2025）、Zhuo 等（2022）和 Gonzalez-Salazar 等（2018）提示，煤电还可能承担容量、灵活性和保供功能，且低碳替代具有系统成本。Shi 和 Shen（2018）关于煤炭产能控制的非预期后果，也说明退出政策若脱离供给、价格和市场制度，可能制造新的风险。因此，本文的政策含义应是“退出需要替代服务和制度安排”，而非“所有煤电应立即退出”。这恰好使“调整—退出缺口”成为一个电力系统设计问题，而不是立场表达。

### 4. 中国政策与绿色金融文献表明：政策密度高，但退出责任并未自动被分配

中国绿色信贷文献记录了银行环境风险管理和绿色投资动员的制度演进，也注意到执行不均与微观传导识别的困难（Aizawa & Yang, 2010；Zhang et al., 2011；CBRC, 2012）。绿色金融的一般理论同样支持其对投资方向、融资成本和转型风险的潜在影响（Campiglio, 2016）。但绿色信贷指引并不是煤电机组退出方案：它不能单独规定存量机组的关闭日期、业主债务如何处置、替代容量在哪里形成，或煤炭地区的财政和就业损失由谁承担。因此，没有银行—业主—机组层面信贷与债务数据时，不能把 2012 年后的省级变化解释为绿色信贷导致了退出或导致了未退出。

更广泛的政策文件呈现出一个密集而多目标的政策组合：大气污染治理、电力市场改革、煤电规划风险预警、可再生能源消纳责任、双碳行动、煤电节能降碳/灵活性/供热改造、现代能源体系建设与煤电容量电价机制，分别涉及清洁运行、可再生能源吸收、系统安全和经济激励。它们支持本文的制度背景判断：2012–2023 年的政策架构包含了强烈的运行调整和系统转型内容。它们也限制本文的表述：除非逐条引证一手文本，不能断言政策制定者“刻意不退出煤电”。更准确的说法是，这些工具本身并不能自动完成机组退役、替代容量、退出融资和地方调整四项任务的协调。

### 5. 公正转型与区域转型研究把“退出”从企业决策变成区域治理问题

区域转型和公正转型研究指出，化石能源退出的成本具有高度空间集中性。资源型地区的就业、税收、国有资产和产业关联，使退役不只是一个机组或企业层面的现金流决策（Hansen & Coenen, 2015；Newell & Mulvaney, 2013；Healy & Barry, 2017）。Jiang 等（2023）对中国省级煤炭依赖的评估，说明省际脆弱性不能由煤炭产量或煤电规模单独概括；Diluiso 等（2021）总结不同煤炭退出案例，也强调多层级协同。Green 和 Gambhir（2020）进一步将过渡援助拆分为对象、工具与时点。

这条文献线为本文 Figure 3 的价值提供了更准确的定位。该图不应被包装成“资源依赖导致煤电不退出”的因果检验，而应被视为政策分型工具：煤电存量、可再生能源消纳、资源依赖、可靠性需求和项目管线的组合，意味着不同省份需要不同的退出与替代包。由此，本文的地方财政与劳动者讨论也不能停留在原则层面，而应成为第二代政策设计的一个组成部分。

### 6. 综合缺口与本文贡献

现有研究已经分别提供了三个强有力的知识块：第一，转型可以通过效率提升、技术扩散和能源结构变化推进；第二，煤电退出需要机组排序、风险分担和政治可行性；第三，中国低碳电力系统依赖储能、输电、调度、市场改革和区域协调。真正缺少的是把三者放在同一个历史证据框架中，判断运行与构成改善是否已经外溢为直接资产退出，以及当这种外溢未被观察到时，政策组合还缺少什么。

本文以 2000–2023 年省级运行/构成数据、全球能源监测机构的项目和机组生命周期记录、以及省级约束条件的描述性证据，提供这一连接。它的贡献不是把绿色信贷识别为单一因果冲击，也不是宣称中国没有发生能源转型；它展示的是一个更窄也更有政策含义的事实：运行调整和构成转型可以与煤电存量持续存在甚至扩张并存。由此，文章把问题从“转型是否发生”推进到“怎样把已经发生的调整转化为有序、可融资、可替代且对地区公平的退出路径”。

## 可直接改写进英文主稿的版本

### Literature review: adjustment, lock-in, and the missing exit pathway

Research on sustainability transitions explains why decarbonization rarely unfolds as a simple replacement of one technology by another. Carbon lock-in arises from mutually reinforcing technological, institutional, and political arrangements (Unruh, 2000), while increasing returns and policy feedback can make initially reasonable policy trajectories difficult to reverse (Pierson, 2000). Transition scholarship consequently recognises multiple pathways of change rather than a single linear sequence (Geels & Schot, 2007; Markard et al., 2012). Environmental and financial policy can redirect innovation, investment, and operating practices (Acemoglu et al., 2012; Campiglio, 2016). These insights establish why lower coal intensity, tighter pollution control, and renewable expansion are meaningful transition outcomes. They do not, however, establish that the stock of incumbent coal assets has exited. A coal unit may run fewer hours, improve its heat rate, comply with tighter pollution standards, or be supplemented by renewable generation while remaining operational. This distinction motivates an analytical separation between operating adjustment, compositional change, and asset exit.

A growing coal-phaseout literature addresses the exit margin directly. Plant-level and scenario-based studies quantify the operational-lifetime constraint that coal fleets face under Paris-consistent pathways and identify potential retirement sequences for China (Cui et al., 2019, 2021). Other work links early retirement to just-transition outcomes, identifies politically sensitive intervention points, and compares mandatory phaseout instruments with carbon-market policies (Zhang et al., 2022; Heerma van Voss et al., 2022; Yu et al., 2023). This literature makes clear that coal exit requires more than a general preference for low-carbon investment: it requires decisions over retirement timing, allocation of debt and compensation, remediation, replacement services, and constraints on new high-carbon investment. Studies of stranded assets and transition feasibility further show that these decisions distribute costs across owners, lenders, workers, local governments, and consumers (Johnson et al., 2015; van der Ploeg & Venables, 2020; Muttitt et al., 2023). Yet most contributions ask which plants should retire in a normative or modelled pathway. Less attention has been paid to whether an observed period of cleaner operation, renewable deployment, and environmental-financial constraint has in fact generated accelerated retirement of incumbent coal assets.

The Chinese power-system literature supplies an essential qualification to any exit argument. Decarbonization depends on a portfolio of replacement and integration resources, including storage, transmission, hydro flexibility, dispatch reform, and renewable absorption (He et al., 2016, 2020; Liu et al., 2019). In a coal-heavy grid, dispatch and grid rules influence whether variable renewable generation substitutes for thermal output rather than merely adding capacity (Davidson et al., 2016), while reductions in curtailment reflect both system and policy change (Chen et al., 2022). China’s wind and solar potential is considerable (Wang et al., 2023), but a lower thermal generation share does not itself determine the disposition of a coal unit. Coal may continue to supply capacity, flexibility, or reliability services, and the cost of providing equivalent low-carbon services can be material (Gonzalez-Salazar et al., 2018; Zhuo et al., 2022; An et al., 2025). The relevant policy question is therefore not whether all coal units should close immediately, but whether a credible replacement package permits an orderly change in their role and eventual exit.

This distinction also clarifies the role of green finance and Chinese transition policy. China’s green-credit framework embeds environmental risk in lending practice and can influence lending conditions, retrofit investment, and renewable investment (Aizawa & Yang, 2010; Zhang et al., 2011; CBRC, 2012). It does not, by itself, specify a closure date for an existing generator, resolve owner debt, provide replacement capacity, or compensate coal-dependent localities. The broader policy mix similarly contains instruments directed at pollution control, power-market reform, renewable consumption, coal-unit upgrading, system security, and capacity remuneration. These policies constitute a substantial transition architecture, but their presence should not be conflated with a fleet-wide exit programme. Without bank-to-owner or bank-to-plant lending and debt data, an empirical analysis cannot attribute either operating adjustment or the absence of retirement acceleration to the Green Credit Guidelines alone.

Finally, regional-transition research shows why asset exit cannot be treated as a plant-level engineering decision. Coal dependence is embedded in local employment, fiscal revenue, state-owned assets, and industrial linkages, and these conditions vary markedly across regions (Hansen & Coenen, 2015; Newell & Mulvaney, 2013; Jiang et al., 2023). Comparative coal-transition evidence and the transitional-assistance literature therefore stress coordinated measures for affected workers, places, and institutions (Diluiso et al., 2021; Green & Gambhir, 2020). Building on these literatures, this study does not claim that China lacks energy-transition policy, nor does it treat post-2012 provincial variation as a clean green-credit experiment. It instead uses direct coal-unit histories alongside provincial operating, compositional, and constraint measures to diagnose whether adjustment spilled over into asset exit. The contribution is to show how real progress at the operating and composition margins can coexist with continued coal-asset accumulation, thereby revealing a policy-design problem: the absence of an integrated retirement, replacement, finance, and local-adjustment pathway.

## 写作与证据边界

- 情景模型和逐厂退出排序用于说明“可行退出路径需要什么”，不能被写成本文历史结果的验证。
- 政策文件用于界定政策工具和制度背景；对具体目标、条款和执行程度，主稿必须在下载并存档一手文本后再写精确表述。
- 绿色信贷部分应保留为制度背景和研究设计边界，不能重新扩张为本文的主因果故事。
- Figure 3 是描述性分型；其最合适的文献语言是 *policy typology* 或 *diagnostic profile*，不是 causal classification。
